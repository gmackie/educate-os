Get the EducateOS app

EducateOS on your phone shows your classes, assignments, grades and messages from your school.

The app is in testing and is not in the App Store or Google Play yet. Your school will tell you how to install it. Once it is installed, scan your school's code again.

Came here from sign-in?

Open the EducateOS app and start sign-in again. If this page keeps appearing, ask your school to check its mobile sign-in setup.
