Professional contact and request

Adults and professional details only. Do not enter identifying student or pupil data, names of children, student records, grades, credentials, secrets, links or URLs. Describe a workflow in general terms. This form cannot guarantee automated detection of sensitive information.

How we use your details

Gmacko Ventures LLC, the current project operator, uses your professional contact details to review this request. Permission to respond covers this specific request. Ongoing updates require the separate optional checkbox. Nothing here sends email automatically or enrolls a school.

Requests are held as unverified until a person verifies email control and organizational affiliation. Self-reported roles are not proof of authority. Authorized reviewers may manually download requests to a private review tool. We do not sell these details.

Contact records expire after 90 days. We keep a keyed, non-plaintext address suppression marker to honor opt-outs. A reviewer must verify address ownership before erasure. Opt out with only your email, or reply “unsubscribe” to any future update; no login or confirmation email is required. You can also request deletion using this form or contact hello@educateos.io.

The security check uses Cloudflare Turnstile and sends your IP address and challenge response to Cloudflare. It loads only when intake is open. No advertising pixels or behavioral tracking are added. See the site privacy policy.
