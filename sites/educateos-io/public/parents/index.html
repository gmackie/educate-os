For families

Your child's school week, at a glance.

Once your school invites you, you see your own child's classes, what's due, released feedback, official grades and announcements. A weekly email sums it up, and your child's private work stays private.

What you see

Enough to help, never more than you should.

  • Your child's classes this term, and their finished classes.
  • Assignments and due dates, feedback the teacher has released, official grades and announcements.
  • Not your child's drafts, their personal space or their conversations.
  • Only your own children. A school administrator confirms every family link.

Privacy

The school keeps the data. We don't.

Student records stay in the district's own Cloudflare account. There is no advertising, no sale of data and no behavioral profiling. Questions about your child's data go to the school, which controls it.

Built for FERPA and COPPA

EducateOS is designed to support your school's privacy obligations. It isn't a certification, and we don't claim one.
