Ownership

Your account, your data, your domain if you want it.

Runs in the district's Cloudflare account

Cloudflare OS deploys into an account you own. EducateOS keeps that model: the Workshop, the district app, the gatekeepers and their storage all live in the district's account.

Available in Cloudflare OS

educateos.io stays out of the data path

educateos.io offers a guided install, updates and a marketplace. It never stores or inspects school data, holds no standing credentials for your account, and never decides which features you can use. A district can install and update without it, and can serve EducateOS on its own domain.

In development

Open source, Apache-2.0

The code that runs in your account is public, so your IT team or an outside reviewer can read exactly what it does. There is no paid tier inside the platform.

Available in Cloudflare OS
Controls

Nothing is allowed until a rule allows it.

Sandboxed agents and gadgets

Each agent and gadget starts with access to nothing. Gadgets run in a sandbox that can't reach the internet except through the gatekeepers a person gave it.

Available in Cloudflare OS

Approvals for writes

Gatekeepers queue every action with side effects for a person to approve. In EducateOS, grades and other school records that an agent drafts stay drafts until a teacher approves them.

Approval queue: Available in Cloudflare OSTeacher-approved grades: In development

A default-deny school policy

One rule table decides every school read and write, using who the person is, their role and their enrollments. If no rule allows a request, it's denied. The agent's identity always comes from sign-in, never from what a tool was asked.

In development

Hall Monitor AI screening

Before a model sees a new message, the AI call guard sends it to Hall Monitor and applies the decision. Where a district's profile requires it, model replies are held and screened before a student sees them.

In development

AI spend caps

Every model call holds its maximum cost against the person's allowance on the district's own ledger, then settles at the actual cost. Districts set the limits. Safety checks come from a separate protected pool.

In development

An audit trail

Gatekeepers log every action and every read an agent or gadget makes. EducateOS adds a record of each AI call, attributed to a person, a class and a feature.

Action log: Available in Cloudflare OSAI call records: In development

Signed releases, verified offline

Every release is signed. An installation checks the signature against keys it has pinned, without calling home, before it applies an update.

In development

Cloudflare Access sign-in

Staff and students sign in through Cloudflare Access with the identity provider the district already uses. The district app turns that verified sign-in into a person in its directory.

Student privacy

Built for FERPA and COPPA.

Student education records stay in the district's own Cloudflare account. Gmacko Ventures LLC, which develops EducateOS, doesn't receive, store or process them, unless a district asks in writing for hands-on support under its own control. The design is meant to support the district's own FERPA, COPPA and state student-privacy obligations. It is not a certification, and the district still makes its own review.
