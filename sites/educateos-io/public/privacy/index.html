Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains what Gmacko Ventures LLC ("we", "us") collects through educateos.io, www.educateos.io, educateos.dev and www.educateos.dev, and the services we offer there. It is short because we collect very little.

District installations are not covered here

EducateOS is open-source software that a school district, school or co-op installs in its own Cloudflare account. That installation, and all the student and staff data in it, belongs to the district. It is governed by the district's own policies and agreements, not by this policy. We don't receive, store or process education records from installations. To ask about data in a school's EducateOS, contact the school or district. Our student privacy page describes how the software is designed.

What we collect

Visiting this website

Apart from the operator console described next, the site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies for visitors and runs no advertising or tracking scripts. Today it loads no analytics. If we add Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and doesn't track individual visitors, we'll update this policy first. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes request information such as IP addresses and browser details to deliver and protect it, as described in Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Signing in to the operator console

People who install or manage EducateOS through educateos.io ("operators") sign in at educateos.io/app with Cloudflare Access, using a one-time code sent to their email address or their GitHub account. When an operator signs in, Access sets sign-in cookies on educateos.io and on its own sign-in page; visitors who never sign in get none. If an operator chooses GitHub, GitHub tells Access their email address. We keep the email address Access verified, the user ID Access assigns, and when the operator first signed in and was last seen signed in, so we can show each operator their own installations. Cloudflare also records each sign-in (the email address, IP address, country and time) in our Cloudflare account's Access logs. We never receive a password.

Emailing us

Mail to hello@educateos.io is forwarded through Cloudflare Email Routing to a mailbox Gmacko Ventures LLC operates. We keep what you send, including your name, address and message, so we can reply and follow up. Please don't email us student records.

Guided install (when available)

The guided install will ask you to authorize a one-time Cloudflare OAuth token for your account. We use it only to deploy EducateOS into your account and then discard it. We keep no standing access.

Installation registration (opt-in)

An installation can choose to register with educateos.io. It is off by default. Registration and its status reports carry a fixed list of fields: an installation ID and public key, a district label, the release version, enabled capabilities, a contact role, an account reference, timestamps, health-check results as status codes, and optionally one district-wide AI spend total. They never carry prompts, chat text, student work, rosters or anything about an individual.

Opening EducateOS from Clever

When someone opens EducateOS from their Clever portal, Clever sends their browser to educateos.io/clever/launch with a one-time code. We exchange the code with Clever for a short-lived token, use it once to read which Clever district the person belongs to, and send the browser on to that district's EducateOS address, where the district signs them in through Clever itself. We don't store, log or pass on the code, the token, or the person's Clever IDs.

Managed address (when available)

If a district uses an address such as district.educateos.io, we keep the name and the target it routes to. Requests to that address pass through Cloudflare under our zone for routing and TLS. We configure no logging, storage or inspection of their content.

Billing (when available)

Paid services such as the managed address, guided install, support and marketplace items will be billed through Stripe. We'll receive the purchaser's name, email and billing address, what was bought and the payment status. Stripe processes card details under its own privacy policy, and we never see full card numbers.

Marketplace (when available)

We'll keep a record of which organization obtained which marketplace items. Items are delivered as signed files that the installation verifies itself. Installations don't report their use of items back to us.

How we use it

  • To answer you and provide the services you ask for.
  • To bill for paid services, once they exist.
  • To keep the site and services secure and working.
  • To meet legal obligations.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent personal information.
  • We don't use it for advertising, and we don't build behavioral profiles.
  • We don't receive student education records from installations.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run these services: Cloudflare (hosting, operator sign-in, email routing and DNS), our email provider, and Stripe once billing exists. Each processes the information only to provide its service to us. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to a successor if the business is transferred, in which case this policy continues to apply to it.

Children's privacy

This website is meant for educators, administrators and families researching EducateOS. It isn't directed to children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, email hello@educateos.io and we'll delete it. Students use EducateOS through their school's own installation, and that data is covered by the school's policies, as described above.

How long we keep it

We keep correspondence for as long as it's needed to answer you and for our records, and billing records for as long as tax and accounting law requires. We delete installation registrations when the installation unregisters, and managed-address records when the address is released. We keep an operator's sign-in record while they use the operator console, and delete it on request to hello@educateos.io.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop using it. Depending on where you live, for example in California, you may have further rights under state law. We won't treat you differently for using them. Email hello@educateos.io and we'll respond within 30 days.

Security

We keep what we collect to a minimum, use the access controls of the services above, and keep no standing credentials for any district's account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use information we already hold, we'll say so on this page before it takes effect.
