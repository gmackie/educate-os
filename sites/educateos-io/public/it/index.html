Architecture

Your account runs everything. educateos.io is optional.

educateos.io (optional)

  • Serves signed release files and a guided install
  • An optional address at district.educateos.io; your own domain works too
  • Holds no standing credentials for your account, and never sees school data
  • A district can install, update and run with educateos.io offline

Your district's Cloudflare account

District app: courses, rosters, grades, families and administration (a Worker storing its records in a SQLite Durable Object)
Workshop: teacher agents, gadgets and AI tutors, each sandboxed
Gatekeepers: the only way an agent reaches an outside service, with approvals for writes
AI call guard: Hall Monitor screening and spending caps on every model call
Cloudflare Access in front, with the identity provider you already use
  • Student records are stored here, under your credentials and policies
  • Apache-2.0 code: your staff can read exactly what runs
Install and permissions

One command, one token you create and can delete.

An administrator runs npx launch-educate-os on their own machine. It verifies the release against pinned signing keys, then links to Cloudflare's token editor already filled in with the permissions below. The token stays on that machine. Deleting it ends the installer's access.

PermissionWhy the installer needs it
Account Settings: Read · Billing: ReadConfirm the account and that its plan supports what EducateOS deploys
Workers Scripts: Edit · Workers Routes: EditDeploy and update the EducateOS Workers, their secrets, schedules and custom domains, and roll back
D1: Edit · Workers KV Storage: Edit · Workers R2 Storage: EditCreate the district's databases, key-value stores and file buckets
Access: Apps and Policies: Edit · Access: Organizations, Identity Providers, and Groups: ReadPut Cloudflare Access in front of every EducateOS address, using your existing login methods
Zone: Read · DNS: Read and EditFind your domain and add the records your EducateOS address needs
SSL and Certificates: EditOnly for an educateos.io address
AI Gateway: EditOptional: an AI Gateway for the district's model calls
Evidence. A token with exactly these permissions completed a full install into a live Cloudflare account. The one-command installer has installed EducateOS end to end into the project's own account. No district has run it yet, and a timed install into a clean account is still to come. The install guide says which recorded run each time comes from. The permission record lists every API call and how far each one has been verified.
Your existing systems

Sign-in, rostering and your LMS.

Sign-in

Staff and students sign in through Cloudflare Access with the identity provider you already use. People are matched by a stable ID from the identity provider, never by email alone. Google sign-in runs on the live demo district today. Clever sign-in is being validated against Clever's live sandbox.

Validating against live sandbox

Rostering

Clever Data API and OneRoster 1.2 sync with a preview before every apply, quarantine for conflicting identities, deactivation rather than deletion, and a hold on any unusually large removal until an administrator approves it. Tested against faithful fakes of each service, with live sandbox runs to follow.

LMS

EducateOS is an LTI 1.3 tool with Deep Linking, grade passback and roster services. Grades go back only after a teacher approves them. It passed live runs against current Canvas and Moodle 4.5. We make no 1EdTech certification claim.

AI controls

Say yes to AI on your terms.

  • Every model call goes through the AI call guard in your account. Hall Monitor screens messages before the model sees them.
  • Each call reserves its maximum cost against a person's allowance on the district's own ledger, so spending has hard caps that the district sets.
  • One default-deny policy decides every school read and write. District policy floors can't be loosened by a school or class setting.
  • Grades and other records an agent drafts stay drafts until a teacher approves them.
For pilots. We suggest starting with teacher-facing AI. Student-facing AI helpers are still in development, and a district can switch them off entirely.

Student privacy

We don't hold your students' records.

Student education records stay in your district's Cloudflare account. Gmacko Ventures LLC, which develops EducateOS, doesn't receive, store or process them unless your district asks in writing for hands-on support, under your control. EducateOS is built to support your FERPA, COPPA and state student-privacy obligations. That is a design, not a certification, and your district still makes its own review.
