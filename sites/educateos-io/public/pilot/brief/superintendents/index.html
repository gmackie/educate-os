EducateOS
Founding pilot brief · For superintendents and school boards
November 2026
educateos.io/pilot · hello@educateos.io

Say yes to AI for teachers, and keep the district's records in the district's hands.

EducateOS is an open-source learning platform with AI built in, installed in your district's own Cloudflare account. We are inviting three to five public districts in Texas and Michigan to a free, ten-week founding pilot that starts with AI for teachers only.

The problem it solves for you

  • Teachers are already using AI tools the district never reviewed, with no record of what goes into them.
  • Most AI products for schools send student data to the vendor's servers, on the vendor's terms.
  • AI spending is hard to predict, and a board needs a ceiling it can see.

What EducateOS is

One platform for classes, assignments, quizzes, a gradebook and family updates, plus an AI workspace where teachers get course agents and small sandboxed classroom apps. Every AI call passes through a guard in your account that applies district policy, screens messages and enforces spending caps. The code is open source under Apache-2.0, so your staff can read exactly what runs.

What the pilot asks of the district

  • A district sponsor, one school, a school lead and three to six champion teachers.
  • A few hours of IT time before kickoff, and a 20-minute check-in each week.
  • Its own Cloudflare bill, paid directly to Cloudflare (below).
  • Honest feedback: two short teacher surveys and the issues you hit.

What the district gets

  • The software free, and a guided install and pilot support at no charge.
  • Teacher training in week 1 and written success criteria agreed at kickoff.
  • A written end-of-pilot review. Then you decide: keep it, export or uninstall.
  • A seat on the founding advisory councils and a direct say in what we build next.

Cost

The software is free and open source, with no per-seat fee to us. The district pays Cloudflare directly. Our projected running cost is about $8.09 a month at 500 students and $37.29 at 2,500, at list prices. A teachers-first pilot of up to 50 people fits Cloudflare's free sign-in plan. AI model usage is billed by the model provider, up to the cap the district sets. Optional educateos.io services after the pilot are not priced yet; you will hear prices before anything is charged.

Who holds student data

Student records stay in the district's Cloudflare account, under the district's credentials and policies. Gmacko Ventures LLC, which develops EducateOS, does not receive them. Until the district signs a data processing agreement, the pilot uses synthetic or staff-only data, and student-facing AI stays off. Your IT lead can stop all access at any time without us. EducateOS is designed to support your FERPA, COPPA and state obligations; we make no compliance or certification claim, and your district makes its own review.
