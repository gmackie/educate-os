EducateOS
Founding pilot brief · For district IT and technology directors
November 2026
educateos.io/pilot · hello@educateos.io

Runs in your Cloudflare account, under a token you create and can delete.

EducateOS is an open-source (Apache-2.0) learning platform with AI built in. You install it in your district's own Cloudflare account with one command; educateos.io is optional and never in the data path. The founding pilot: one school, teachers first, about ten weeks, in three to five Texas and Michigan districts.

The problem it solves for you

  • Every new vendor is another copy of student data outside your control.
  • Staff are using unreviewed AI tools, with no logs, no screening and no spending limit.
  • You need to be able to read what runs, cap what it costs, and remove it cleanly.

What EducateOS is

Workers in your account: the district app (courses, rosters, grades, families) in a SQLite Durable Object, and an AI workspace where each agent and gadget is sandboxed. Gatekeepers are the only way an agent reaches an outside service. Every model call passes an AI call guard: district policy, Hall Monitor screening, spending caps. Cloudflare Access sits in front, with your identity provider.

What the pilot asks of you

  • A Cloudflare account the district owns, on Workers Paid (from $5 a month), with R2 and a domain as an active zone.
  • Cloudflare Zero Trust with your identity provider (Google, Entra ID, Clever, or SAML/OIDC) as a login method.
  • An API token with the 13 permissions the install guide lists, created by you and deleted after.
  • A few hours before kickoff, then npx launch-educate-os with us on a call (Node.js 22.12+).

What you get

  • A guided install, free, and a technical contact who fixes or tracks every issue.
  • The evaluation packet: permission record, security questionnaire, DPA template, accessibility report.
  • The stop switch: change the Access policy in your account and access ends. It does not need us.
  • At the end: keep it, export everything as open JSON, or uninstall.

Cost

No license fee. Cloudflare bills you directly; our projection at list prices is about $8.09 a month at 500 students and $37.29 at 2,500, Workers Paid base included. Access seats are separate: up to 50 people fit Zero Trust Free, enough for a teachers-first pilot. AI usage is billed by your model provider, up to the cap you set.

Who holds student data

Student records live in your account, under your credentials. Gmacko Ventures LLC holds no standing credentials for your account and does not receive student records. Signed releases, verified against keys you pin, are in development. Until a DPA is signed the pilot uses synthetic or staff-only data, and no real roster is connected. Designed to support FERPA, COPPA and state obligations; no compliance or certification claim is made.

Next step. Email hello@educateos.io for a 20-minute IT walkthrough. Tell us your identity provider, roster source and LMS, and we'll tell you honestly how ready EducateOS is for each. Evaluation packet: educateos.io/it. Full terms: educateos.io/pilot.
