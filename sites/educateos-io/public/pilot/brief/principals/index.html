EducateOS
Founding pilot brief · For principals and school leads
November 2026
educateos.io/pilot · hello@educateos.io

Give a few teachers better AI tools this term, on terms your district controls.

EducateOS is an open-source learning platform with AI built in, installed in your district's own Cloudflare account. The founding pilot runs in one school for about ten weeks, with a few champion teachers and AI for teachers only. We are inviting three to five public districts in Texas and Michigan.

The problem it solves for you

  • Teachers lose evenings to building practice sets, differentiating lessons and drafting quizzes.
  • Some already paste lesson material into AI tools nobody at the school has reviewed.
  • New tools usually mean a new login, a new contract and training nobody has time for.

What EducateOS is

Teacher Studio for courses, assignments, quizzes and a gradebook, with 12 open courses to start from, plus an AI workspace where teachers get course agents and small sandboxed classroom apps ("gadgets"). Nothing reaches students until a teacher approves it, and grades an agent drafts stay drafts until the teacher approves them.

What the pilot asks of you

  • A school lead (you, or an instructional lead) who picks three to six champion teachers.
  • An hour of training for each of them in week 1, and a fixed 20-minute check-in each week.
  • One or two problems worth solving, such as differentiating a unit, to anchor success criteria.
  • Sharing the pilot's data rule with every champion teacher before they sign in.

What you get

  • Week-1 training and printable kits: teacher quickstart, staff AI guidance, student AI posters and a family letter in English and Spanish.
  • Weekly support, and a reply within one business day by email.
  • Written success criteria, reviewed at week 5 and week 10.
  • A real say in what gets built next.

Cost

There is no cost to your school's budget for the software or our support in the pilot. The district pays Cloudflare directly for hosting: projected at about $8.09 a month for 500 students at list prices, and a teachers-first pilot of up to 50 people fits Cloudflare's free sign-in plan. AI usage is capped by the district.

Who holds student data

Everything runs in the district's own Cloudflare account; Gmacko Ventures LLC, which develops EducateOS, does not receive student records. Until the district signs a data processing agreement, the pilot uses synthetic or staff-only data: teachers' own accounts and teaching materials, never students' names, work or grades. Student-facing AI stays off. We make no compliance or certification claim.

Next step. Ask your district office or IT lead to email hello@educateos.io, or write to us yourself with your school and district. We reply within one business day. Try the demo at educateos.io/demo; full terms at educateos.io/pilot.
