EducateOS
Founding pilot brief · For champion teachers
November 2026
educateos.io/pilot · hello@educateos.io

AI that helps you plan and build, with you as the final say.

EducateOS is an open-source learning platform with AI built in, run by your district in its own account. In the founding pilot a few champion teachers in one school use it in their own planning for about ten weeks, and tell us honestly what works.

The problem it solves for you

  • Building practice sets, quizzes and differentiated versions of a lesson takes hours you don't have.
  • General AI chat tools don't know your course, and you can't tell where what you paste ends up.
  • You want help with the busywork without anything reaching students before you check it.

What EducateOS is

Teacher Studio for modules, assignments, six types of quiz question and a weighted gradebook, with 12 open courses you can start from. Beside it, an AI workspace: an agent for your course that reads only the resources you give it, and small sandboxed classroom apps ("gadgets") such as flashcards, a simulation or a lab plotter, built by asking. Emails, documents and grades an agent prepares wait for your approval.

What the pilot asks of you

  • About an hour of training in week 1, in the demo district.
  • Using it in your own planning each week, on problems you choose.
  • A 20-minute check-in each week, and two short surveys.
  • Keeping to the data rule: no student names, work or grades in it until the district signs a DPA.

What you get

  • Free access during the pilot. You pay nothing, and neither does your school budget.
  • A printable quickstart, staff AI guidance and student AI posters for your room.
  • Help within one business day, and someone who fixes or tracks every issue you raise.
  • A direct say in what gets built next, and a seat on the educator advisory council if you want one.

Cost

Nothing for you or your school budget. The software is free and open source, and the district pays its own small hosting bill to Cloudflare. The district sets a cap on AI usage; when a budget runs out, the assistant stops and your work carries on without it.

Who holds student data

EducateOS runs in your district's own Cloudflare account, and the company that develops it does not receive student records. During the pilot you use your own staff account and your own teaching materials only: no student names, work or grades until the district signs a data processing agreement. If something an agent produces looks wrong or unsafe, you report it and the district can stop access at once. No compliance or certification claim is made.
