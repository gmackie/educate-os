For district leaders and IT · Founding pilot

The founding pilot

We are looking for three to five public school districts in Texas and Michigan to run EducateOS with us: one school, a few champion teachers and about ten weeks, starting with AI for teachers. The software is free, we install it with your IT team in your own Cloudflare account, and we agree in writing at kickoff what success looks like.

Program summary, November 2026. This page summarizes the program; it is not a contract. The signed pilot agreement and the data processing agreement govern. The editable source is docs/educateos/pilot/program.md.

Who it's for

  • Public school districts in Texas and Michigan. Three to five districts in this first round.
  • One school in each district, with a school lead who wants to try it and a few champion teachers (we suggest three to six) who will use it each week.
  • An IT team willing to install it in the district's own Cloudflare account, with our help on a call.
  • Teacher-facing AI first. Teachers use agents and gadgets to plan and build classroom materials. Student-facing AI stays off in the pilot.
Where EducateOS is today. EducateOS is early. It runs today on synthetic demo districts, and no real students use it yet. No district has run the installer in its own account yet, so the founding pilot districts will be the first. Every capability on this site carries the status its evidence supports, from the project's maturity record. We make no compliance or certification claims.

What the district gets, and what it gives

The district gets

  • The software, free. EducateOS is open source under Apache-2.0, with no per-seat fee to us.
  • A guided install and pilot support at no charge. We run the installer with your IT team on a call, in your account, under a token you create and delete.
  • Training for the champion teachers in week 1, and a 20-minute check-in each week after.
  • Written success criteria agreed at kickoff, and a written end-of-pilot review against them.
  • A seat on the founding advisory councils as we form them, for an educator, an IT lead or both. See the advisory councils.
  • A direct say in what gets built next. Issues you raise go into the project's tracker, and we tell you what happened to each one.

The district gives

  • Its own Cloudflare bill, paid directly to Cloudflare. Workers Paid starts at $5 a month plus small usage, and up to 50 people signing in fits Zero Trust Free. AI model usage is billed by the model provider, up to the cap the district sets. Nothing passes through us. See the cost to run.
  • Staff time. A few hours of IT time before kickoff, about an hour of training for each champion teacher, regular use in their own planning, and the weekly 20-minute check-in.
  • Honest feedback. A short baseline survey and exit survey for the champion teachers, and the issues you run into, as you run into them.
  • A reference at the end, if the district is willing. It is never a condition of the pilot, and we never name a district publicly without its written approval.
After the pilot. The software stays free and open source. The optional services from educateos.io, such as the guided install after the pilot and managed updates, will be priced later; pilot districts will hear the prices before anything is charged.

The ten weeks

Before kickoff

Readiness and install

  • A 20-minute IT walkthrough
  • The readiness checklist below
  • The pilot agreement signed; the DPA's status agreed
  • Guided install on a call, then verification
Week 1

Kickoff and teacher training

  • Kickoff with every role
  • Success criteria written and agreed
  • Training for the champion teachers
  • Baseline survey
Weeks 2 to 9

Classroom use

  • Teachers use it in their own planning
  • A 20-minute check-in each week
  • An issue log kept by both sides
  • A mid-point review in week 5
Week 10

Review

  • Exit survey
  • Review against the success criteria
  • The district decides: keep, export or uninstall

The readiness weeks depend most on your domain and identity provider: with them already on Cloudflare, the install itself takes under an hour. School calendars move the dates; we agree them at kickoff.

Readiness checklist

For IT

  • A Cloudflare account the district owns, with a payment method, and an administrator who can create an API token in it.
  • Workers Paid on that account (from $5 a month), with R2 turned on.
  • A domain as an active zone in the account, with no DNS records yet at the hostname you'll use (for example educateos.yourdistrict.org).
  • Cloudflare Zero Trust with a team, and the district's identity provider (Google Workspace, Microsoft Entra ID, Clever or another SAML or OIDC provider) added as a login method.
  • Who may sign in: the staff email domain or the champion teachers' addresses, and one to twenty administrators. Zero Trust Free admits 50 people, which covers a teachers-first pilot.
  • A machine with Node.js 22.12 or later to run npx launch-educate-os.
  • AI model access: which provider the pilot uses, who holds that account, and the monthly AI cap. Before any teacher signs in, we confirm with you in writing which AI controls (screening and spending caps) are in place in your install.
  • Your roster source, if any (Clever, ClassLink, OneRoster, Google Classroom or none). A teachers-first pilot does not need rostering, and no real roster is connected before a DPA is signed.
  • Your LMS, if any (for example Canvas, Moodle, Schoology or Google Classroom). We'll tell you honestly how ready EducateOS is for it; see integrations.

The full list, with the 13 API token permissions and what the installer's preflight checks, is in the district install guide.

For the school

  • A school lead who can give the champion teachers time for training and check-ins.
  • A few champion teachers who plan lessons each week and want to try AI in that work.
  • An hour in week 1 for training, and a fixed 20-minute slot each week for the check-in.
  • One or two problems worth solving, such as building practice materials, differentiating a lesson or drafting quizzes, to anchor the success criteria.
  • Agreement on the data rule below, shared with every champion teacher before they sign in.
  • The baseline survey answered in week 1, before teachers start using EducateOS.

The data rule

Until the district signs a data processing agreement, the pilot uses synthetic data or staff-only data. Staff-only means teachers' own accounts and their own teaching materials. Nobody enters students' names, records, grades or work, and no real roster is connected.

  • EducateOS runs in the district's own Cloudflare account. Gmacko Ventures LLC, which develops it, does not receive student records.
  • The DPA template is a draft pending counsel review. If your state uses a standard student data privacy agreement, send it to us.
  • Student-facing AI stays off throughout the pilot.
  • Your IT lead can stop all access at any time by changing the EducateOS Access policy in your account. It doesn't need us.

Success criteria

At kickoff the district sponsor, the school lead and we write down three to five measurable criteria, with a target for each and how it will be measured. We review against them at week 5 and week 10. Examples for teacher-facing AI:

  • Teacher time saved per week, self-reported in the check-ins and surveys.
  • The number of champion teachers active each week.
  • Classroom materials (assignments, quizzes, gadgets) created with EducateOS and used with students.
  • Teacher satisfaction in the exit survey.
  • Zero unresolved safety escalations at the end of the pilot.

Some of these numbers come from your own account (sign-ins in Cloudflare Access, AI usage), and some only from teachers. The success criteria worksheet says which is which.

Support

  • Email hello@educateos.io with "Pilot" and your district's name in the subject. Our support target during the pilot is a reply within one business day.
  • The weekly 20-minute check-in covers what worked, what didn't and the issue log.
  • Safety or data concerns come first. Put "Safety" in the subject. If something looks wrong, your IT lead can stop access in your account straight away and then write to us.
  • The support target is a pilot commitment, not a service-level agreement. The pilot agreement says what we commit to.

The pilot kit

Printable materials for the school. Each one has an editable source in the repository.

Teacher quickstart

Sign in, classes, assignments, timed quizzes with extended time, the gradebook, the AI tools and getting help. One task per printed page.

Kickoff training

A 60-minute session for champion teachers, with hands-on tasks in the demo district and facilitator notes.

Family letter

A letter announcing the pilot, in English and Spanish, with fields for your school to fill in.

Student AI guidelines

Plain-language rules for K–2, 3–5, 6–8 and 9–12, each printable as a classroom poster.

Staff AI guidance

What staff can use AI for, what never goes into it, reviewing output and what to do when a request is flagged.

Playbook and worksheets

The kickoff agenda, weekly check-in, success-criteria worksheet and teacher surveys.

What happens at the end

Keep it

EducateOS keeps running in your account, under your control. The software stays free. A district moving on to students needs a signed DPA first, and seat-free student sign-in is still planned.

Export

A district administrator downloads the district's records as one ZIP of open JSON records and files, and each student can download their own archive. Teachers' Workshop gadgets and agents are not in either export yet. Tested against a seeded district, not yet run in a live one.

Harness-tested

Uninstall

Delete everything named educateos-<name>-* in your account, following the offboarding runbook. There is no uninstall command yet. We help on a call if you want us to.

Whatever the district decides, we write up the end-of-pilot review against the success criteria and send it to the district sponsor.

How to apply

  1. Email hello@educateos.io to book a 20-minute IT walkthrough. Tell us your district, the school you have in mind, your identity provider, your roster source and your LMS.
  2. We reply within one business day and tell you honestly how ready EducateOS is for your systems.
  3. If it fits, we send the pilot agreement and the DPA template, and agree a kickoff date.

Professional details only, please: no student information in email.
