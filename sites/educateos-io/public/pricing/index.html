Cost to run

What EducateOS costs your district to run.

EducateOS runs in your district's own Cloudflare account, so you pay Cloudflare directly for what it uses. This page shows the running cost by enrollment size, then the two things outside it: Cloudflare Access sign-in seats, which many districts already have, and the AI spending cap you set. Every figure is labeled as measured or projected, and the method is public.

Founding pilot terms

The software is free. You pay your own Cloudflare bill.

Open source

EducateOS is Apache-2.0. Every educational feature is in the open core, with no paid tier inside the software and no per-seat fee to us.

Guided install and support, free in the pilot

During the founding pilot we install EducateOS with your IT team and support it, at no charge.

Your Cloudflare bill, paid directly

EducateOS runs in your Cloudflare account. Cloudflare bills you, and nothing passes through us. The tables below estimate that bill.

Not priced yet. The optional services from educateos.io (a managed district.educateos.io address, the guided install after the pilot, managed updates and the marketplace) will be priced later. We have not set those prices, so none appear here. Pilot districts will hear about them before they apply.

Sign-in seats

Access seats: separate from the running cost.

People sign in to EducateOS through Cloudflare Access, with your district's own identity provider behind it. Many districts already have Cloudflare Zero Trust, and when EducateOS is installed in the account that holds that plan, sign-ins use its seats, so we show seats apart from the running cost rather than adding them in. Cloudflare's Zero Trust Free plan covers 50 people. Above that, the published pay-as-you-go price is $7 per person per month, for everyone, and a person holds a seat from their first sign-in until you remove them. The seats column in the table above counts every student and teacher, and a guardian for half of the students.

AI model usage

Your AI cap, not a fixed cost.

AI model calls are billed by the model provider through your account, and EducateOS's AI call guard keeps a ledger of every call against the budgets you set: a district pool, a protected safety reserve and a cap for each person. When a budget is used up, the assistant stops and the work carries on without it. So the most AI can cost you each month is the cap you choose, and it is shown separately from the bill above.

You set the cap

District, school and per-person budgets, set in the admin console. Nothing is spent past them.

Teachers first

Where the numbers come from

Measured where we could, assumed where we had to.

We read 30 days of Cloudflare analytics (2026-10-14 to 2026-11-12) for the live demo districts, which run the same district Worker you would install. They have synthetic rosters and little traffic, so how much people use EducateOS is our assumption. What each request and each background job costs is measured. The full method, the raw measurements and the calculation are in cost-to-run.md, and the inputs are in cost-to-run.json.

What we found while measuring. At 10,000 students and above, the largest usage line is database rows written, and about half of them come from the hourly notification sweep, which today writes a few rows per student every hour even when nothing has changed. A fix to make it write only what changed is planned. The district also keeps its records in one Durable Object, which Cloudflare limits to 10 GB and about 1,000 requests a second, so the largest sizes here need a design change before a district that size can run on it.
