Gadgets and blueprints

Ask for an app. Share it as a blueprint.

In Cloudflare OS, a gadget is a small app the agent builds when you describe it, running in its own sandbox. A blueprint shares the gadget's code, so another teacher or school can make their own copy and connect it to their own classes. Here are gadgets a school could build today with connectors and MCP servers.

How blueprints work

Share the app, never the data.

Available in Cloudflare OS
  • A blueprint carries the gadget's code and a description of each connection it needs. It never carries the gadget's stored data, its chat history or anyone's credentials.
  • Anyone with the link can see what a blueprint does and what it connects to. Making a copy means signing in and connecting your own classes and accounts.
  • When the author publishes a new version, each copy can preview the update and choose to take it. Nothing changes automatically.
  • Administrators can feature blueprints for everyone, such as Docs, Sheets and Slides, and a district can ship its own set with its installation. Blueprints can also be exported as a file and imported into another installation.

A marketplace of signed blueprints and curriculum packs on educateos.io is Planned.
