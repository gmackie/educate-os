How we validate

Fakes first, then the vendor's sandbox.

Every integration is tested first against fakes: a fake LTI platform that signs launches and rotates or withdraws its keys, fake Advantage services that check scopes, a synthetic district's OneRoster export and REST API, and Classroom API fakes. Durable stores are also tested on the real Workers runtime. Next comes a run against each vendor's sandbox, with pilot districts.
