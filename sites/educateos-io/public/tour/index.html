Product tour

EducateOS, screen by screen.

Every image here is a real screenshot. Most come from the zero-login demo, which runs the real district application in your browser for Northfield Public Schools, a fictional K-12 district. The AI tutor screenshots come from the live demo district, signed in as synthetic students.

Students by grade band Teachers Families District office AI tutors The demo
Students

Built for the grade, from first grade to high school.

A first grader answers by speaking and drawing, one step a screen, with every instruction read aloud. Older students get read-aloud on request, rubric feedback and official grades once the teacher releases them.

Teachers

A semester ready to edit, and grades that wait for the teacher.

Every showcase class has its whole semester from one of the 12 open courses. Teacher guides and answer keys come with each unit and are visible to teachers only.

Families

One sign-in for every child, and only what teachers share.

Rosa Delgado follows Mateo in grade 1 and Lucía in grade 10, in two schools. She sees classes, announcements, released feedback and official grades, never a child's drafts or private space.

District office

Every class, standard and course in one console.

The administrator sees the whole district: people, rosters, classes from kindergarten to grade 12, the course library, which learning standards are approved, policies and the AI budget.

AI tutors

Four tutors, each screened at its grade band.

Captured on the live demo district, with real AI behind the AI call guard and Hall Monitor, signed in as synthetic students. Pilot preview · synthetic students only

No real student uses EducateOS or its tutors yet. Turning student AI on is the district's decision, inside the spending limits it sets. How the guardrails work →
