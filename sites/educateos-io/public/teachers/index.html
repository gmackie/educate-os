For teachers

Your course, your rules. The busywork, delegated.

Teacher Studio covers the course itself: modules, activities, quizzes, a gradebook and discussions, with a semester of open course material ready to edit. Cloudflare OS agents build the small apps a lesson needs. Nothing reaches students until you approve it.

Teacher Studio

A complete course toolkit, with you as the final say.

  • Modules and pages, activity templates, and Publish and assign.
  • A whole semester from one of 12 open courses, added as drafts you edit. Teacher guides and answer keys stay teachers only.
  • Activities for every age, from voice recordings and drawings in grade 1 to essays and data tables.
  • Common Cartridge import and export, including the QTI quizzes inside.
  • Quizzes with six question types and auto-scoring.
  • A gradebook with weighted groups, drop-lowest, excused and missing marks, and CSV export.
  • Rubric scoring and automatic code checks. A grade becomes official only when you approve it.
