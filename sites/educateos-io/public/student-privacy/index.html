Student privacy

Built for FERPA and COPPA.

Student education records stay in the district's own Cloudflare account. Gmacko Ventures LLC, which develops EducateOS, never receives, stores or processes them, unless a district asks in writing for hands-on support under its own control. EducateOS is designed to support the district's FERPA, COPPA and state student-privacy obligations. That is a description of the design, not a certification, and each district makes its own legal review.

FERPA

The district stays the custodian of education records.

EducateOS is software the district runs itself. There is no vendor-held copy of student records to request, breach or subpoena from us.

Direct control, no vendor copy

The district installs EducateOS into an account it owns and operates. Under FERPA's school-official framing, the district keeps direct control of the records and their use. In the normal course, Gmacko Ventures LLC has no access to them. If a district ever asks us for hands-on support that involves records, that happens only with the district's written authorization and under its control, as the DPA template sets out.

Your own account: Available in Cloudflare OS

Guardians see only their own children

Guardian access comes only from a relationship a district administrator has confirmed. Guardians are read-only, never see more than the child can, and never read a student's personal workspace, conversations or submissions. Revoking a relationship never deletes it, so the history stays auditable.

In development

An audit trail without content

Every policy decision is written to an append-only log that records who, what and the outcome as IDs, never the content itself. If the audit record can't be written, the request is refused. Identity links, guardian changes and deletions have their own audit records.

In development

Retention the district sets

The district chooses how long a closed course's workspace is kept, from 30 to 3,650 days with a default of 365. After that it becomes eligible for deletion. A legal hold blocks deletion, and each deletion leaves a tombstone and an audit record. Students' personal workspaces are never removed by this process.

In development

Ending a term never deletes work

Dropping a student or closing a term revokes access but deletes nothing. Roster removals deactivate people rather than deleting them, and enrollments are kept as history.

In development

Export and portability

A student archive format carries a learner's own work, and a signed transfer between districts needs consent. Teachers can export a course as a Common Cartridge. The student export isn't available in the district app yet.

In development
COPPA

For students under 13, the school decides, and the district holds the data.

Under COPPA, a school can authorize the collection of a child's information for educational use. EducateOS is designed for that model. The district, not an outside vendor, runs the system and collects the data, and only for the school's own purposes.

School authorization, not self-sign-up

Students get access from the district's roster and sign in through the district's own identity provider, behind Cloudflare Access. People the district hasn't added see a clear screen saying so, rather than a way to sign themselves up.

In development

The district controls student AI

The default policy limits student AI to hints, with a $2 monthly cap per person. A district can turn student AI off entirely, and teacher-facing AI features start switched off. Hint mode is an instruction to the model, so Hall Monitor also screens messages, and replies where the district requires it.

In development

No advertising, no sale, no profiling

EducateOS contains no advertising. Gmacko Ventures LLC doesn't sell student data, and has none to sell. Automated social-emotional profiling is prohibited in every policy profile, and no school or class setting can turn it on.

Profiling prohibition: In development

Data minimization

The Google Classroom connector doesn't request email addresses, photos or guardian data, and roster entries carry only an ID and a name. Email addresses are never used as identity keys. By default, prompts are kept out of AI Gateway logs.

In development
State student-privacy laws

The same design properties, whatever your state.

Laws such as California's Student Online Personal Information Protection Act (SOPIPA) limit what operators may do with student information: no targeted advertising, no selling it, no profiles for purposes other than school, and reasonable security. The properties above are how EducateOS is designed to meet that kind of rule. The data stays in the district's account, there is no advertising or sale, profiling is prohibited, and the district controls retention and deletion.

We describe the design rather than claim compliance with any particular statute. Your counsel decides how it maps to your state's law, and we'll answer any questions they have.

What we don't claim

No certificates, no audit badges.

EducateOS holds no FERPA, COPPA or state-law certification, because none of those laws certifies software. It hasn't had an independent privacy or security audit, and it isn't a signatory to any student-privacy pledge. EducateOS is in development, and most of the controls above have not yet run in a real district.
