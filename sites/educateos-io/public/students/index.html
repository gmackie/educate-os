For students

One calm place for school, and work that stays yours.

EducateOS gives students a home for every class: what's due, the work itself, quizzes, discussions, feedback and grades. When a class ends, the work doesn't disappear.

What you get

Less hunting, more doing.

  • A Learning Home with your courses, announcements and your own space.
  • A calendar of due dates, announcements and term dates across your classes, with a feed for any calendar app.
  • Write, record your voice, draw, upload files or fill in a data table, then submit and get a receipt code.
  • Feedback and grades appear when your teacher releases them.
By grade band

Screens that fit the student's age.

Grades K–2

Speak and draw

One step a screen, read aloud, answered with a microphone and a drawing pad.

Grades 3–5

Hints, not answers

Read-aloud on request, and a helper that gives sentence starters, not the work.

Grades 6–8

Feedback you can use

Rubric scores and the teacher's comment, released to you and your family.

Grades 9–12

Official grades, clearly

Released feedback, rubric scores and the official grade in one place.

Your privacy

No ads. No selling. No profiles.

EducateOS has no advertising, Gmacko Ventures LLC doesn't sell student data, and automated social-emotional profiling is prohibited in every school policy. Your school keeps your data in its own account.

What your family can see

If your school invites a parent or guardian, they see your classes, due dates, released feedback, official grades and announcements. They don't see your drafts, your own space or your conversations.
