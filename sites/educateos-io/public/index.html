A learning platform your district owns.

EducateOS is a K-12 learning platform that every school district, school or homeschool co-op runs in its own Cloudflare account. Teachers get courses, assignments and a gradebook. Students get a safe place to learn, with optional classroom AI. Student data stays in the district's own account.

Everything a classroom needs

A complete learning environment, without the need to buy and stitch together several products.

Courses and assignments

Modules, pages, due dates, submissions, quizzes with auto-scoring, and Common Cartridge and QTI import.

Gradebook

Weighted totals and teacher-approved official grades. AI never sets a grade.

Families

Guardians see their own children's work through confirmed relationships, with announcements, a calendar and a weekly digest.

Safe classroom AI

Optional AI help for students and teachers, screened by district policy, with spending caps the district controls.

Discussions

Course discussions with moderation tools built for K-12.

Standards-aligned curriculum

Curriculum packs aligned to published standards frameworks, which districts can adapt.

The district owns the system and the data

  • Each district's installation runs in its own Cloudflare account. EducateOS is never in the data path.
  • No feature is paywalled inside the platform. The code is open source under Apache-2.0.
  • Releases are signed, and the district verifies them against keys it pins.
  • Each installation can use its own domain, or a default address at district.educateos.io.

Works with the tools schools already use

Integrations under development and testing:

OneRoster 1.2, Clever and ClassLink, with previews and safe sync. Dropping an enrollment never deletes a student's work.

Classes, coursework and grades synchronized with Google Classroom.
