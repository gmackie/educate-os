● Cloudflare OS Built on the open-source Cloudflare OS

A K-12 learning platform the district owns outright.

EducateOS is courses, a gradebook, a family view and a district console, with AI tutors screened at each student's grade band. It is open source and installs into the district's own Cloudflare account, so no vendor sits between students and their work.

Kindergarten to grade 12

The same product for a six-year-old and a sixteen-year-old.

Each grade band gets screens built for it: one step at a time and read aloud for the youngest, primary sources and rubric feedback in middle school, argument essays and official grades in high school. These are the demo's real screens.

Grades K–2

Answer by voice and drawing

One step a screen, big type, Read to me on every instruction, a microphone and a drawing pad. No typing or reading required.

Grades 3–5

Read-aloud and hints, not answers

Instructions read aloud on request, and a Homework Helper that gives hints and sentence starters and won't do the graded work.

Grades 6–8

Primary sources, scored on a rubric

Sourcing, context and a claim with evidence, with the teacher's comment and rubric scores released to the student and family.

Grades 9–12

Arguments with evidence, official grades

Released feedback, rubric scores and the official grade in one place, and an essay coach that won't write the essay.

Teachers

Recordings and drawings arrive as work

Teachers score on rubrics written for the grade, and a score becomes the official grade only when they approve it.

Families

What the teacher has shared, never more

One guardian follows a first grader and a tenth grader in two schools, and never sees a child's drafts or private space.

District

Every class in one console

Rosters, guardians, learning standards, the course library, policies and AI budgets for the whole district.

AI tutors with guardrails

A tutor for every grade band, screened before the AI sees a word.

Reading Buddy, Homework Helper, Study Coach and the Research & Essay Coach ship with EducateOS. Hall Monitor screens every message at the student's grade band: asking for the answers, sharing personal details or wandering off topic is redirected, and a blocked message never reaches the model. Spending is capped by the district.

Pilot preview · synthetic students only

These were captured on the live Northfield district, with real AI behind the AI call guard and Hall Monitor, signed in as synthetic students. Student AI is a pilot preview on synthetic identities only: no real student uses it, and turning it on for students is the district's decision. What Cloudflare OS brings to a school

The same ideas that make Cloudflare OS safe at work, tuned for a classroom.

Cloudflare OS was built so a whole company could use AI without keeping its security team up at night. EducateOS keeps that design and adds what schools need: classes, families, age-appropriate guardrails and a gradebook.

Agents that know the course

Each class gets a workspace. Its agent can see the approved curriculum, the class calendar and the teacher's materials, and only what the district's policy allows.

Lesson planningClassroom gadgetsCurriculum context

Gadgets, built by asking

Teachers ask for small apps such as a simulation, a flashcard deck or a lab data plotter. Each one runs in a sandbox and can be shared as a blueprint. Student access to gadgets is planned.

SandboxedShareableExportable

Gatekeepers and Hall Monitor

Every tool call, data read and grade write passes through one policy that denies by default. Student-facing AI is screened, spending is capped, and writes wait for a person to approve them.

A learning management system, built in

Everything a class needs, so a co-op or small school doesn't need a separate LMS.

Districts with Canvas, Moodle or Google Classroom can keep them, and EducateOS works alongside. Schools without an LMS get one included, at no extra cost.

District-owned and open source

The district owns the system and the data.

EducateOS installs into the district's own Cloudflare account. educateos.io offers installs, updates, the course catalog and the marketplace, but it never sits between students and their work.

educateos.io

  • Guided install and signed updates
  • Default address at district.educateos.io, or use your own domain
  • Open courses, gadgets, curriculum packs and LTI tools
  • Optional: districts can run everything without it

Bring EducateOS to your district.

Start with the demo: it runs the real district application in your browser, with no sign-in. When you're ready to run your own, EducateOS installs into your Cloudflare account.

$npx launch-educate-osComing soon

The installer that sets up EducateOS in a district's own Cloudflare account from one command is being built and isn't published yet. Until it is, use the guided install on educateos.io (sign in with a verified email), or tell us about your district and we'll walk through it with you. No district has run an install yet; the live demo district runs the same code, deployed from source.

Run it in a terminal with Node.js 22.12 or later.
