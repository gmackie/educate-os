What Cloudflare OS brings to a school

The same ideas that make Cloudflare OS safe at work, tuned for a classroom.

Cloudflare OS was built so a whole company could use AI without keeping its security team up at night. EducateOS keeps that design and adds what schools need: classes, families, age-appropriate guardrails and a gradebook.

Agents that know the course

Each class gets a workspace. Its agent can see the approved curriculum, the class calendar and the teacher's materials, and only what the district's policy allows.

Lesson planningFeedback draftsHint-only tutoring

Gadgets, built by asking

Teachers, and students when allowed, ask for small apps such as a simulation, a flashcard deck or a lab data plotter. Each one runs in a sandbox and can be shared with a class.

SandboxedShareableExportable

Gatekeepers and Hall Monitor

Every tool call, data read and grade write passes through one policy that denies by default. Student-facing AI is screened, spending is capped, and writes wait for a person to approve them.

Default-denyTeacher approvalAI spend caps

For students

One calm home for everything due, with a helper that teaches rather than tells.

  • What's due across every class, from EducateOS, Google Classroom or Canvas, in one list.
  • An AI helper in hint mode, which can't hand over answers, if the district and teacher turn it on.
  • Work that belongs to the student: it's versioned, never deleted when a class ends, and exportable.

LMS-lite, built in

A complete learning management system, so a co-op or small school doesn't need a separate one.

Districts with Canvas or Google Classroom can keep using them, and EducateOS works alongside. Schools without an LMS get one included, at no extra cost.

📚

Courses & modules

Pages, files and modules, with Common Cartridge import and export.

📝

📈

Integrations

Fits the systems a district already runs.

Rosters come from the student information system. Coursework and grades flow to whichever LMS is the official record. Each connection shows its health, and a large or unexpected change waits for an administrator instead of applying silently.

  • Rostering: OneRoster 1.2, Clever and ClassLink
  • LMS: Canvas and Schoology through LTI 1.3 Advantage, plus Google Classroom
  • Sign-in: Google, Microsoft, Clever and ClassLink, through Cloudflare Access

These integrations are in development and are being validated against demo districts. EducateOS claims no 1EdTech certification.

Bring your own account

The district owns the system and the data.

EducateOS installs into the district's own Cloudflare account. educateos.io handles installs, updates and the marketplace, but it never sits between students and their work.

educateos.io

  • Guided install and signed updates
  • Default address at district.educateos.io, or use your own domain
  • Marketplace for gadgets, curriculum packs and LTI tools
  • Optional: districts can run everything without it

Your district's Cloudflare account

🧠 Workshop: agents, gadgets and workspaces
🏫 District app: courses, rosters, grades and families
🛡️ Gatekeepers, policy and the Hall Monitor AI guard
🗄️ Durable storage, backups and audit log
  • Student data never leaves the account
  • No paywalled features inside the platform, and Apache-2.0 code
