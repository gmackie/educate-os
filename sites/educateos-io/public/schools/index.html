Who it's for

One platform, sized for a district or a twelve-family co-op.

EducateOS installs the same open-source software for every kind of school. What changes is what you connect to it: a district brings its roster and LMS, while a co-op can start with nothing but the built-in classes and gradebook.

Districts

Keep your SIS and LMS. Add agents your policy governs.

  • Rosters from OneRoster 1.2 or ClassLink, or Clever, with large or unexpected changes previewed first.
  • Works beside Canvas or Google Classroom, which stay the official gradebook.
  • One default-deny policy, AI screening and spend caps that the district sets centrally.
  • Runs in the district's own Cloudflare account. Student privacy →

Charter and independent schools

A full learning platform without a separate LMS contract.

  • Courses, assignments, quizzes, a gradebook, discussions and a family view are built in.
  • Google sign-in and Google Workspace connectors for a school that already runs on Google.
  • No per-seat fee inside the software. You pay Cloudflare for what your account uses.

Homeschool co-ops

Mixed-age classes, parent teachers and a weekly plan, with no IT department.

  • A co-op admin creates a class, adds a parent as its teacher, enrolls students of different ages and publishes a weekly plan. No LMS or AI is needed.
  • Parents see only their own children's work.
  • When a term ends, students keep a copy of their work. Exporting it is in development.

CS and maker classes

Students build real software, with a teacher in the loop.

  • Gadgets: a teacher asks the agent for a small app and can share its code as a blueprint. Students building and changing gadgets themselves is planned.
  • GitHub and GitLab for real repositories, with pushes and merges queued for approval.
  • Supabase for a databases unit, and read-only Cloudflare logs and metrics when students deploy Workers.
  • Automatic checks on submitted code, with feedback the teacher reviews.
