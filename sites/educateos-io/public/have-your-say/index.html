Better tools start
with listening.

What should education technology make easier — and what should it leave alone?

EducateOS is an open-source project exploring tools that give educators and families more control over their work and data. We want to learn from teachers, school leaders, parents, homeschool educators and people who are skeptical of AI or new platforms.

A foundation model is proposed. The foundation is not registered as a nonprofit. Gmacko Ventures LLC currently operates EducateOS. Participation is not a purchase, donation or commitment to use a product; pricing and future services are not promised.
