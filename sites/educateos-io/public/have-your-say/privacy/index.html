How this adult-only listening exercise handles your answers and optional contact details.

For adults aged 18 and over. No children may participate. Do not submit student names, records, grades, work, health information or other sensitive data. No login required.

Who is responsible?

Gmacko Ventures LLC is the current operator and data controller for this listening exercise. Contact hello@educateos.io for privacy questions, deletion requests or to withdraw follow-up permission. A foundation is proposed; it is not a registered nonprofit. This notice makes no claim of certification or guaranteed legal compliance.

What we collect and why

The survey collects only the choices you select about your adult perspective, current education tools, workflow needs, AI policy preferences, possible involvement and barriers. It also records your adult declaration, participation agreement, notice version, a random record ID and submission time. There are no name, email, school, location or free-text fields in the survey. All six questions are optional.

The separate follow-up form records your email, selected discussion topic, explicit follow-up permission, adult declaration, participation agreement, notice version, random record ID and submission time. We use it to respond about that topic. It is not linked to your survey answers and is not marketing consent. We do not verify your identity or role from these declarations.

Participation and use

Taking part is voluntary and limited to people aged 18 or over. Do not submit information about children or student records. Skip questions, leave without submitting, or complete only the optional follow-up form. We use answers to understand needs and inform EducateOS planning. Authorized staff may review individual answers privately and prepare combined findings. Individual submissions and contact details are not published. Small groups will be combined or omitted from any public findings to reduce identification risk. No automated eligibility, education or purchasing decisions are made from responses.

Security checks and analytics

These listening pages use no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, session replay, cookies or browser storage for responses. Cloudflare hosts the site and durable storage and processes network information to deliver and protect it. When collection is open, Cloudflare Turnstile processes a security challenge and your IP address to deter automated submissions. Our application stores keyed hashes for short-lived rate limits and challenge replay checks, not raw IP addresses or challenge tokens. These protections do not make a survey absolutely anonymous: combinations of answers or submission timing can sometimes identify someone.

Access, retention and processors

Only authorized EducateOS reviewers receive private exports. Cloudflare provides hosting, security checks and storage; processing may occur outside your country. We do not sell responses or send them into a marketing system. Submitted records and retry identifiers expire after 90 days. Expired records are immediately excluded from exports and the hourly cleanup removes them from active storage. Abuse rate counters last up to two days, challenge replay hashes about six minutes, and aggregate bot counters seven days. A keyed suppression marker remains until this listening exercise and its associated follow-up activity end so a deleted submission is not reintroduced.

Authorized reviewers must keep exports on private encrypted storage, remove them when the 90-day period ends or a verified deletion request arrives, and restrict access to the research purpose. Cloudflare recovery copies may temporarily retain deleted data according to its service settings; operators must check recovery retention before opening collection and apply deletions after a restore. We do not promise immediate removal from all backups.

Request deletion or stop follow-up

After submitting the survey, save the deletion code displayed on screen. To request removal, email hello@educateos.io with the subject “Listening privacy request” and that code. The code lets the team locate your answers without collecting your identity. It is not included in exports. Without it, we may be unable to find your particular survey response.

For the separate contact form, email from the address you submitted to request deletion or withdraw permission. The team may verify control of that address before erasing records. No extra consent is required to stop follow-up. Staff must remove matching private exports as well as active records. Do not send student records or unnecessary identification documents. We will respond about handling the request; this notice does not set or replace any statutory rights or deadlines.

Collection remains closed until the published notice, operational contact, security settings, retention and staff procedures have been verified. If the form says closed, it has not accepted your answers.
