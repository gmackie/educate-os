Cloudflare OS for schools

Agents, gadgets and guardrails for K-12, in the district's own account.

EducateOS turns the open-source Cloudflare OS into a learning environment. Classes get agents that know the course, teachers and students build small sandboxed apps, and every data read and write passes through a policy the district sets. It is open source (Apache-2.0) and in development.

What it is

  • Agents for each class, limited to what policy allows.
  • Gadgets: small apps built by asking, each in its own sandbox.
  • Gatekeepers: connectors where reads are recorded and writes wait for a person.
  • LMS-lite: courses, assignments, quizzes, gradebook and families.

Who owns it

  • Installed in the district's Cloudflare account, under its credentials.
  • educateos.io offers an optional guided install, updates and a marketplace. It never sits in the data path or gates features.
  • Signed releases, verified offline against keys the district pins.

Student privacy

  • Built for FERPA and COPPA. Education records stay in the district's account. Gmacko Ventures LLC never receives, stores or processes them, outside support a district requests in writing.
  • No advertising, no sale of data, and automated profiling prohibited.
  • No certification or third-party audit is claimed.

Good fits for a pilot

Districts that keep their SIS and LMS and add governed agents. Charter and independent schools that want a learning platform without a separate LMS contract. Homeschool co-ops with mixed-age classes and parent teachers. CS and maker classes using GitHub, Supabase and their own Workers.
