Keep provider responsibilities precise

Clever supplies prospective roster, identity and launch context. Google or another LMS supplies coursework, content, submissions and grades. No Clever grade-write or content API has been established. Clever SSO does not grant Google OAuth authority, and roster enrollment does not grant Google file access.

Teacher presentation gadgets retain teacher authority. Students must use separate personal resources and instances. The local practice quiz saves no responses and sends no grades.
