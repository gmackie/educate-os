# Pilot kickoff: 60-minute teacher training

- Issue: SCHO-398. Pilot: SCHO-366.
- Published as <https://educateos.io/pilot/teachers/training/> from
  `sites/educateos-io/public/pilot/teachers/training/index.html`. This file is the editable source:
  change both together.
- Companion handout: the [teacher quickstart](teacher-quickstart.md)
  (<https://educateos.io/pilot/teachers/>). Print one per teacher.
- The hands-on tasks were done in the public demo on 2026-11-14 (Northfield, as Mr. Rivera). The AI
  segment is from the code; the public demo has no AI workspace.

For the person running the founding-pilot kickoff with the school's champion teachers: an
EducateOS team member, the school's pilot lead, or both. Everything hands-on happens in the public
demo, so no one touches real student data.

## Before the session

- **The demo works on the school's devices and network.** Open <https://educateos.io/demo/> on a
  teacher laptop on the school network. It is a large page (over 3 MB of script) that runs entirely in
  the browser, in a current Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. If a filter blocks it, ask IT to allow
  `educateos.io`.
- **Everyone can sign in to the pilot district.** Ask the district administrator to add each champion
  teacher and assign their classes before the day. Anyone who sees "You're signed in, but you haven't
  been added to this school yet" sends their sign-in ID to the administrator.
- **Know whether AI is on.** Ask the district whether its AI workspace is turned on, which models it
  allows, and its monthly cap per person. If it is on, sign in to `/ai` as yourself and make sure a
  short request works. If it is off, the AI segment is a walk-through only.
- **Name the pilot lead** and how teachers reach them. Write it on the quickstart's last page.
- **Print** the quickstart, one per teacher, and this page's checklist.
- **Pick personas.** One demo teacher per participant, closest to what they teach:

| Persona | Class | Link straight to it |
|---|---|---|
| Daniel Yazzie | Grade 1 Reading and Grade 1 Mathematics | <https://educateos.io/demo/?persona=k2-teacher> |
| Fatima Al-Sayed | Grade 4 ELA and Grade 4 Mathematics | <https://educateos.io/demo/?persona=g35-teacher> |
| Tomás Ortega | World History 7 | <https://educateos.io/demo/?persona=g68-teacher> |
| Sam Rivera | English 9 | <https://educateos.io/demo/?persona=g912-teacher> |

Each laptop runs its own copy of the demo, so participants don't see each other's changes.
**Reset demo** starts over; so does reloading the page, which means a reload mid-task loses the work.
**Things to try** in the demo's banner lists each persona's own tasks and ticks them off.

### 0:00 Welcome (5 min)

Say:

- The pilot is one school, a few teachers, about ten weeks. We want your honest reaction, including
  what's confusing or missing.
- **The data rule.** Until the district signs the data processing agreement, we use made-up students
  and staff accounts only. No real student names, work or grades, in Teacher Studio or in an AI prompt.
- The pilot starts with AI for teachers. Student AI helpers are still in development, and the
  district can keep them off.
- Today is all in the public demo, a fictional district that runs in your browser.

### 0:05 Open the demo (5 min)

Everyone opens their persona's link. Point out:

- The **Demo** banner: fictional district, everything stays in your browser, AI tutors are simulated.
- **My classes** and a class card. Open it and read the tabs: Roster, Modules, Resources, Create
  activity, Submissions, Gradebook, Announcements, Discussions, Quizzes.
- **Roster**: who's enrolled and how much they've turned in. The district's roster decides who is in
  a class.

On the real district, a teacher signs in and lands in Teacher Studio. The menu adds Today, Classes,
Gradebook, Calendar, Inbox and, when AI is on, AI workspace.

### 0:10 Hands-on 1: post an assignment (12 min)

Each participant, in their class:

1. **Create activity**. Pick a template that fits (Writing task for most; Picture response or Read and
   respond for K-2).
2. Fill in **Title**, **Overview for students** and the template's fields.
3. **AI help**: talk through None, Hints only and Full. The district may allow less.
4. **Rubric**: choose "The rubric from …" an earlier activity, or create a simple one.
5. **Preview as student**, read it, then **Publish and assign** with a **Due date**.
6. Open **Submissions** and find the new activity.

Watch for: a box titled "A few things to finish" lists what is missing, usually an empty required field. Leaving the due date
empty makes it due at the end of the term.

### 0:22 Hands-on 2: a timed quiz with extended time (12 min)

1. **Quizzes**, type a **Quiz title**, **Create quiz**.
2. **Time limit in minutes (optional)**: enter 20.
3. Add two questions (for example **Add true or false** and **Add multiple choice**); fill each prompt
   and correct answer.
4. **Save quiz**, then **Publish**.
5. Scroll to **Extended time**. Pick a student, pick **1.5×** (it shows "1.5× time (30 minutes)"),
   **Give extended time**.

Talk through: only the class's teachers and district administrators see the list; each student sees
only their own time; an attempt in progress gets the extra time at once; extended time needs a time
limit. Once a student starts, the questions lock and settings stay editable.

### 0:34 Hands-on 3: grade, then read the gradebook (8 min)

Use the persona's own task (it's in **Things to try**):

| Persona | Submission to grade |
|---|---|
| Mr. Yazzie | Retell The Little Seed: **Review Priya Natarajan's work** (recordings and a drawing) |
| Ms. Al-Sayed | The Theme of "The Kite That Would Not Fly": **Review Hana Suzuki's work** |
| Mr. Ortega | What Do Hammurabi's Laws Tell Us?: **Review Felix Brandt's work** |
| Mr. Rivera | Should Community Service Be Required to Graduate?: **Review Elena Sato's work** |

Choose a level for each criterion and **Save score**, add a comment, **Approve official grade**, then
**Share feedback with student**. Point out these are two decisions. Then open **Gradebook**: official
grades only, the new quiz and activity as columns, a cell's **Excused**, **Missing** and **Late** marks,
**Gradebook settings** and **Export CSV**.

### 0:42 Your AI tools (10 min, facilitator demonstrates)

The public demo has no AI workspace, so demonstrate on the pilot district if its AI is on, from your
own account and with no student information. Otherwise walk through the quickstart's AI page.

Show:

1. **AI workspace** in the menu, opening at `/ai` on the same address. Its home, **Teaching
   companion**: **Start a conversation** and the starters **Plan a lesson**, **Create practice
   questions**, **Adapt an activity** and **Draft a family update**.
2. Run **Plan a lesson** for a made-up class and point out that it asks questions first and drafts for
   you to review.
3. **Ask AI about this** on a student's work page (only where AI is on).
4. Optionally, in the public demo, a student persona's tutor (for example Amara Okafor with the
   Research & Essay Coach). It is scripted, and under each message shows what Hall Monitor would
   decide: allowed, redirected or held. That's the student side; it shows what screening looks like.

Say:

- The district sets the policy: whether AI is on, which models, the monthly cap. A class can be
  stricter, never looser.
- Every request goes through the AI call guard in the district's own Cloudflare account. Where the
  district requires it, Hall Monitor screens the message, and can screen the reply.
- If something is flagged you get a short neutral message instead of a reply. It doesn't say why or
  quote you, and nothing else happens. Rephrase or carry on.
- The AI doesn't grade, and doesn't send anything to students or families. You decide what to publish.
- Teacher starter blueprints (Week Ahead, Research Kit, Assignment Companion) are in development. Use
  them only if the district has added them.

Don't try to trigger a flag on purpose on the live district.

## Facilitator notes

- Keep the pace by doing each step on the projector a beat ahead, then circulating.
- If someone falls behind, they can skip to the next segment: the demo's classes already have work in
  every state.
- A participant who reloads loses their changes. Re-open the persona link and carry on.
- If a participant's district sign-in fails during the session, take their sign-in ID for the
  administrator and keep them in the demo.
- Write down every question you can't answer and every "where is…?" moment. Send them to
  hello@educateos.io after the session. They are the pilot's best evidence.
- Don't promise features. If it's not in the quickstart, it's "in development" or "not built", and
  say which.

## Common questions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Can I use my real class? | Not yet. Until the district signs the DPA, use made-up students only. Your pilot lead will say when that changes. |
| Who adds my students? | The district's roster. You can't add students in Teacher Studio. |
| Does the AI grade? | No. Suggested scores count only when you save them as yours and approve the grade. |
| Can students see the AI workspace? | The pilot starts with AI for teachers. Student AI helpers are in development, and the district decides whether to turn them on. On an activity, AI help (None, Hints only, Full) is your choice for students; the district may allow less. |
| Who sees my AI conversations? | The district's AI call guard and, where the district requires it, Hall Monitor check each message. The district keeps no per-message record of those checks; administrators see AI spend. EducateOS staff can't reach the district unless the district lets them. |
| What if the AI says my message can't be sent? | It was flagged by screening. Nothing else happens. Rephrase, or tell your pilot lead if it shouldn't have been stopped. |
| Can I turn AI off for my class? | Set AI help to None on each activity. Whether AI is on at all is the district's decision. |
| Where do grades go? | The EducateOS gradebook, official grades only. Export CSV any time. Sending grades to another system, such as Canvas or Google Classroom, is set up by the district. |
| Can I give extended time to one student? | Yes, per quiz: 1.25× to 4× or no time limit. Only teachers and administrators see who has it. |
| Can I change a quiz after students start? | The settings, yes. The questions lock so every attempt is scored the same way. |
| Do families see drafts? | No. Families and students see feedback only after you share it. |
| Is the demo saved? | No. It runs in your browser and resets when you reload or choose Reset demo. |
| Where do I get help? | Your pilot lead first, then hello@educateos.io. |

      ID to the administrator).
- [ ] I can see my classes, or I've told the pilot lead they're missing.
- [ ] I posted an assignment with a rubric and a due date in the demo.
- [ ] I built a timed quiz and gave a student extended time in the demo.
- [ ] I approved a grade and shared feedback, and found it in the gradebook.
- [ ] I know whether my district's AI workspace is on, and that I don't enter student information in it.
- [ ] I know what a "can't be sent" message means and what to do about it.
- [ ] I have the printed quickstart, my pilot lead's contact and hello@educateos.io.
- [ ] I know the date of the first check-in.
