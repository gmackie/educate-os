# Champion teacher surveys

- Status: Template. Use any survey tool the district approves.
- Issue: SCHO-366. Used by: [playbook.md](playbook.md), [success-criteria.md](success-criteria.md).

Two short surveys, about five minutes each. The baseline is answered in week 1 before teachers
start; the exit survey in week 10. Ask for no student information. Answers go to the district and
the EducateOS pilot lead and are reported only as totals or anonymous quotes, unless a teacher
agrees otherwise.

Scales: 1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree, unless stated.

## Baseline survey (week 1)

1. What do you teach? (grade band and subject)
2. In a typical week, about how many hours do you spend planning and making classroom materials
   (lessons, practice, quizzes, differentiated versions)?
3. Which of those tasks would you most like to spend less time on? (pick up to two)
   - Writing lesson plans
   - Making practice sets or worksheets
   - Writing quizzes or assessments
   - Differentiating materials for different learners
   - Finding and adapting resources
   - Other: ____
4. How often do you use AI tools in your teaching work today? (never / monthly / weekly / daily)
5. "I'm confident I can use AI tools well in my planning." (1 to 5)
6. "I'm comfortable with how AI tools handle school data." (1 to 5)
7. What would make this pilot worth your time? (short answer)

## Exit survey (week 10)

1. In a typical pilot week, about how many hours did you spend planning and making classroom
   materials?
2. On average, how much time did EducateOS save or cost you a week? (minutes; negative if it cost
   time)
3. About how many things did you make with EducateOS that you used with students? (number)
4. "EducateOS helped with the tasks I most wanted help with." (1 to 5)
5. "I would want to keep using EducateOS." (1 to 5)
6. "I would recommend EducateOS to a colleague." (1 to 5)
7. "I'm comfortable with how EducateOS handles school data." (1 to 5)
8. "I'm confident I can use AI tools well in my planning." (1 to 5)
9. Did you see any AI output that was unsafe, wrong in a way that mattered, or inappropriate? (yes /
   no; if yes, was it raised in the issue log?)
10. The one thing we should fix first. (short answer)
11. The most useful thing you made. (short answer)
12. May we quote your answers to 10 and 11 without your name? (yes / no)

Three exit questions repeat a baseline question word for word, so the two can be compared: exit 1
with baseline 2 (planning hours), exit 7 with baseline 6 (comfort with data handling) and exit 8
with baseline 5 (confidence with AI tools).
