What districts are weighing

Teachers want AI help now. The district has to answer for it.

Unreviewed tools

Staff paste lesson material, and sometimes student work, into AI tools nobody reviewed, with no record of what went in.

Data leaves the district

Most AI products for schools keep student data on the vendor's servers, under the vendor's terms.

Costs nobody can cap

Per-seat licences and usage-based AI make next year's bill hard to predict or limit.

One more system

Every new tool is another login, another contract, another security review and more training time.

Ask: which of these is most pressing for you?3
What EducateOS is

A learning platform and an AI workspace, behind one sign-in.

Teacher Studio

  • Modules, assignments, quizzes and a weighted gradebook
  • 12 open courses to start from, added as drafts
  • Families' weekly digest and announcements
  • A grade is official only when the teacher approves it

AI workspace

  • Course agents that read only what the teacher gives them
  • Gadgets: small classroom apps built by asking, each in its own sandbox
  • Emails, documents and grades an agent prepares wait for approval

District console

  • People, rosters, classes, families and term rollover
  • Policies previewed as a dry run before they apply
  • AI budgets and per-person caps
  • A full district export

Built on the open-source Cloudflare OS (Apache-2.0). On the demo districts today, the courses and the AI workspace share one address and one sign-in.

Who owns it

It runs in your Cloudflare account. We are optional.

Your district's Cloudflare account

  • The district app: courses, rosters, grades, families, administration
  • The AI workspace, each agent and gadget sandboxed
  • The AI call guard: policy, screening and spending caps on every model call
  • Cloudflare Access in front, with the identity provider you already use
  • Student records stored here, under your credentials

educateos.io (optional)

  • Release files and a guided install
  • No standing credentials for your account
  • Never in the data path; never sees school data
  • You can install, update and run without it

Your IT lead can end all access by changing one Access policy in your account. It does not need us. The code is open, so your staff can read exactly what runs.

Early, open and honest about it.

What you give, what you get

A fair trade, written down at kickoff.

The district gives

  • A district sponsor, a school lead and three to six champion teachers
  • A few hours of IT time before kickoff
  • An hour of training per teacher, then 20 minutes a week
  • Its own Cloudflare bill, paid directly to Cloudflare
  • Honest feedback, and a reference only if it wants to give one

The district gets

  • The software, free and open source, with no per-seat fee
  • A guided install and pilot support at no charge
  • Teacher training and printable kits for staff, students and families
  • Written success criteria and a written end-of-pilot review
  • A seat on the founding advisory councils and a say in what we build

We never name a district publicly without its written approval.

The data rule

  • Until a data processing agreement is signed: synthetic or staff-only data. No student names, records, grades or work, and no real roster.
  • Records stay in your account. Gmacko Ventures LLC, which develops EducateOS, does not receive them.
  • Our DPA template is a draft pending counsel review; send us your state's standard agreement if you use one.
  • At the end: a district export of open JSON records, or an uninstall that shows what it will delete first. Both tested in simulation, not yet run live.
