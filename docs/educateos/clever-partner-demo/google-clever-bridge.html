What Google makes possible, and what Clever could supply for districts.

12 responsibilities

foundation

Account identity and role

EducateOS provides

EducateOS resolves a principal by district, trusted issuer and stable subject. Class roles come from enrollment, not an email match.

Google supplies today

Google OAuth supplies an account identity. Classroom membership supplies teacher/student course roles. Teacher connector OAuth is demonstrated; full district sign-in/roster linking is still open.

Clever: assumption to confirm

Confirm verified Clever subject (multi_role_user_id/sub), district claims and multi-role behavior; provide admin, teacher, student and non-member logins.

Authority and evidence boundary

A Clever identity and a Google identity require verified linking to the same district principal. SSO alone grants no Google content access.

foundation

District, school, term and class structure

EducateOS provides

The local directory models districts, schools, terms, catalog courses and taught offerings.

Google supplies today

Classroom courses represent taught classes. EducateOS configuration supplies schools, grade bands and terms for this roster path. A selected-course list separates districts sharing a Workspace domain.

Clever: assumption to confirm

Enable and confirm districts, schools, terms, courses and sections with district-approved fields. Clever sections should map to taught offerings; catalog courses stay distinct.

Authority and evidence boundary

Google Classroom course and Clever catalog course are different model objects. Do not map them by name alone.

foundation

Who belongs to which class

EducateOS provides

EducateOS uses active student/staff enrollments to authorize class views and operations.

Google supplies today

Classroom teacher/student lists supply course membership. Real single-course reads exist; complete deployed district sync and stable identity acceptance remain open.

Clever: assumption to confirm

Provide users and section teacher/student membership, secondary teacher/staff/multi-role fixtures and a second isolated district.

Authority and evidence boundary

Enrollment is authorization context. It does not grant a file ACL, mailbox, another learner’s work or teacher grading authority.

foundation

Roster preview, apply and removal

EducateOS provides

Both adapters use the shared import pipeline: validation, quarantine, preview, apply and a large-removal hold. Failed runs preserve prior successful state.

Google supplies today

The Google roster path performs complete polling. Its source and district wiring exist; full live preview/apply/scheduled acceptance is pending.

Clever: assumption to confirm

Confirm API version plus roster entitlement. Enable events with documented retention/cursor/removal semantics, or agree complete-refresh cadence.

Authority and evidence boundary

Clever event changes describe roster data, not Classroom submissions or grading. Harness tests are not a real-provider sync result.

foundation

Role-aware launch

EducateOS provides

EducateOS routes the signed-in principal to the district and the appropriate app context.

Google supplies today

Google-connected Workshop sessions and separate Google Classroom pages have real evidence. This does not prove a whole-district Google launch or identity setup.

Clever: assumption to confirm

Confirm shared portal launch/redirect registration, district claims, stable user ID and role choice. Test each role and wrong-district denial.

Authority and evidence boundary

Portal implementation exists, but current live Clever launch acceptance is open. A persona selector in the offline demo is synthetic authentication.

learning

Instructor creation, agents and gadgets

EducateOS provides

Teacher Studio supplies activities, resources, feedback and class tools. Workshop supplies code-mode agents, resource capabilities, gadgets and reusable blueprints.

Google supplies today

The connector reads the real task/rubric and supports app-owned coursework actions. Science and Algebra gadgets render worksheet, slides, local quiz and discussion, with PDF/PPTX exports.

Clever: assumption to confirm

Roster and launch context can put an instructor in the right district/class. Ask whether any separate Clever product adds content context.

Authority and evidence boundary

EducateOS creates the experience. Clever roster access is not a native Google Doc/Slides/Sheets or authoring API. The offline district demo has AI off.

learning

Individual learner work

EducateOS provides

Learning Home supplies versioned learner artifacts/submissions. The separate personal Classroom capability supplies only published work and own submission state.

Google supplies today

Real work/turn-in and 18/20 visibility were demonstrated in Google Classroom. EducateOS student API is deployed; its new personal gadget still needs live OAuth/approval/turn-in acceptance.

Clever: assumption to confirm

Provide verified learner identity and section membership. Keep individual Google OAuth and the one-course capability separate from the district roster token.

Authority and evidence boundary

A teacher presentation filter cannot protect students. Each learner creates their own blueprint instance; upload/answer authoring and grade display are absent from the student connector API.

learning

Teacher review and grade return

EducateOS provides

EducateOS owns assessment decisions, official grades and feedback-release policy for its native activities. External grade publication uses a supported LMS route.

Google supplies today

The teacher-approved Science 18/20 and return were read back from Google and seen by the learner. Google restricts writes to the app’s project-owned coursework. Rubric criterion grades cannot be published through its API.

Clever: assumption to confirm

Confirm whether any additional Clever product covers learning/grade context; otherwise retain Google or an LMS/LTI connector for those operations.

Authority and evidence boundary

Do not promise Clever submission or grade-write APIs. Native EducateOS grading in the synthetic demo is different evidence from a Google grade-return transaction.

learning

Documents, data, calendars and communication

EducateOS provides

Exact resource bindings support reading guides, lab data, native presentations, scoped libraries, agenda and private communication assistants.

Google supplies today

Fourteen resource kinds are declared. Beyond the demonstrated Classroom/gadget story, native Workspace teaching recipes remain unrehearsed. Drive supports metadata/native reads, not general upload/share/delete.

Clever: assumption to confirm

Use district identity/membership to inform intended audiences. Ask which separately approved app/content integrations Clever recommends.

Authority and evidence boundary

Clever consent does not grant Google OAuth scopes or file/calendar/Chat membership. Gmail and whole-account Chat belong outside learner templates.

operations

Guardian access

EducateOS provides

EducateOS invitations, identity approval and a confirmed relationship scope expose a child’s classes and released results, not submissions or unrelated children.

Google supplies today

Guardian relationships are not supplied by the demonstrated Google connector. The synthetic product demo seeds an EducateOS-approved guardian relationship.

Clever: assumption to confirm

Confirm any supported family/relationship product separately, its consent/field contract and role limitations. The current Clever adapter does not import contacts as guardians.

Authority and evidence boundary

Never infer a guardian relationship from a shared email, basic identity scope, contact record or roster membership.

operations

District control, cost and recovery

EducateOS provides

District policies, approved resources/actions, release signatures, feedback visibility and AI-governance mechanisms belong to EducateOS. Educational records stay with the district or its approved provider.

Google supplies today

Real Google approvals demonstrate write control and readback. Full revoke/reconnect/outage and broader Plus acceptance remain open. The offline product demo has no funded AI or provider traffic.

Clever: assumption to confirm

Confirm consent/sharing revocation, token rotation, quotas, event recovery and support ownership; supply negative fixtures to validate our handling.

Authority and evidence boundary

School/district membership is only one policy input. It does not authorize model access, budgets, publication or unrestricted provider data.

operations

Blueprints and safe distribution

EducateOS provides

A blueprint carries reviewed source and required resource shapes. A new instance gets its own storage, history and authorized bindings.

Google supplies today

Teacher formats are demonstrated and source is included. Personal student companion source is implemented; fresh two-student install/update acceptance remains open.

Clever: assumption to confirm

Use supported Clever sign-in/launch to simplify onboarding and select the intended class; confirm any distribution program or review requirement.

Authority and evidence boundary

Do not distribute a live teacher workspace or its credentials. Copying code does not transfer Google file permissions or provider consent.
